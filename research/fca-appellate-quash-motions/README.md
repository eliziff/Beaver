# Premature summary dismissal of an FCA appeal

Research snapshot: 2026-08-29

## Scope

This folder concerns the rare interlocutory motion asking the Federal Court of
Appeal to terminate an appeal before the normal appeal process because it is plain
and obvious that the appeal has no reasonable chance of success and is clearly
bound to fail.

It excludes:

- motions to strike an action or judicial-review application below;
- jurisdictional motions asserting that no statutory appeal lies;
- motions concerning leave to exercise another party's appeal right;
- ordinary disposition of an appeal after briefing and hearing.

## Best request for five memoranda

1. **Martinez, A-239-18, document 17** - HMTQ's written representations supporting
   the successful motion to quash the appeal.
2. **Lee, A-221-17, document 8** - respondents' written representations supporting
   preliminary dismissal because the appeal had no reasonable chance of success.
3. **Breslaw, A-285-04, document 5** - respondent's English written
   representations supporting premature dismissal of the appeal.
4. **Fotinov, A-390-13, document 5** - RBC's written representations seeking to
   strike the notice of appeal as having no reasonable chance of success,
   frivolous, and an abuse of process.
5. **Fotinov, A-390-13, document 9** - appellant's written representations opposing
   that motion.

Breslaw document 7 is also useful: it is the appellant's English opposition to the
preliminary-dismissal motion. Documents 8 and 9 are the parties' further English
submissions after the Court directed them to address whether an appeal bound to fail
should be allowed to continue.

## Doctrinal line

- Breslaw v Canada (Attorney General), 2004 FCA 264
- Sellathurai v Canada (Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness), 2011 FCA 1
- Lessard-Gauvin v Canada (Attorney General), 2013 FCA 147
- Fotinov v Royal Bank of Canada, 2014 FCA 70
- Lee v Canada (Correctional Service), 2017 FCA 228
- Martinez, A-239-18, order dated September 19, 2018
- Bonev v Canada, 2020 FCA 138

Lee explains that the Federal Courts Rules do not expressly authorize an appeal to
be struck at this early stage. The authority rests in the Court's plenary power to
control its own process. Bonev applies the high threshold directly.

No party memorandum in this narrow category was located as an unrestricted public
download. The docketed documents must be requested from the FCA Registry.
