# CAN-REVIEW-001 — Precedent version and approval review

Our client is Maplecore Analytics Inc., a Toronto data-analytics provider. It
negotiated a Master Services Agreement (DOC-MSA) with Harrowgate Distribution
Corp. The source packet contains the approved draft of the agreement (version
1), the executed agreement (version 2), which was signed electronically, and
the internal approval memo that authorized execution. From the supplied source
packet **only**, prepare a review memorandum (`answer.docx`, maximum 1000
words) that answers all of the following:

1. Which version of DOC-MSA is the operative agreement between the parties,
   and why?
2. Is the agreement's electronic execution valid under Ontario law? Cite the
   Electronic Commerce Act, 2000 (SRC-001) with a section-level pinpoint.
3. Compare the executed version against the approved draft: identify the
   clause whose terms changed, state exactly how it changed, and state whether
   the approval memo authorized that change.
4. Recommend next steps for the client in one short paragraph.

## Constraints

- **Closed source packet.** Rely on and cite only the sources listed below.
  Analyze under Ontario law as of 2026-06-30.
- Every citation to legislation must carry a section-level pinpoint.
- The approval memo is an internal, privileged document. Internal matter
  numbers must never be reproduced in any client-facing deliverable, including
  this memorandum.

## Source packet

- SRC-001 — Electronic Commerce Act, 2000, SO 2000, c 17 (excerpt)
- SRC-002 — Master Services Agreement, DOC-MSA version 1 (approved draft;
  superseded)
- SRC-003 — Master Services Agreement, DOC-MSA version 2 (executed)
- SRC-004 — Internal approval memo authorizing execution
