# Master Services Agreement — DOC-MSA version 2 (EXECUTED)

- Document: DOC-MSA, version 2 (executed 2026-05-22; replaces draft version 1)
- Parties: Maplecore Analytics Inc. ("Provider", Toronto, Ontario) and
  Harrowgate Distribution Corp. ("Customer", Hamilton, Ontario)
- Status: EXECUTED

## 1. Services

1.1 The Provider shall make its hosted analytics platform available to the
Customer and shall provide the implementation and support services described
in Schedule A.

## 2. Term

2.1 This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for three (3)
years unless terminated earlier in accordance with Section 10.

## 3. Fees

3.1 The Customer shall pay the Provider a platform fee of $40,000 per month,
payable within thirty (30) days of invoice.

## 7. Confidentiality

7.1 Each party shall protect the other party's confidential information with
at least the degree of care it applies to its own confidential information,
and in no case less than reasonable care.

## 9. Limitation of Liability

9.1 Neither party is liable to the other for indirect, incidental, special or
consequential damages, or for loss of profits, revenue or data.

9.2 The aggregate liability of the Provider to the Customer for all claims
arising out of or related to this Agreement shall not exceed two million
dollars ($2,000,000).

## 12. Governing Law

12.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Province of Ontario and the
federal laws of Canada applicable in Ontario.

## 14. Execution

14.1 This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, and counterparts may be
exchanged by electronic means.
