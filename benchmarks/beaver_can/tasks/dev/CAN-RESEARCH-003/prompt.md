# CAN-RESEARCH-003 — Open-source research memorandum

Psychological detention, investigative detention and s. 24(2). Kind: case-law synthesis across many decisions (criminal procedure and the Charter).

You are assisting Toronto defence counsel. Our client, a 19-year-old Black man, was standing with friends in the fenced backyard of a townhouse at night when three officers walked in without a warrant, asked everyone for identification and what they were doing, told one man to keep his hands visible, and asked our client what was in his satchel; he fled, was tackled, and a handgun and drugs were found. Prepare a research memorandum for supervising counsel answering: (1) at what point, if any, the client was detained within sections 9 and 10 of the Charter, applying the Grant test as developed in R v Le, including how his race and age bear on the reasonable-person analysis; (2) whether any detention was a lawful investigative detention under R v Mann and the reasonable-suspicion standard explained in R v Chehil; (3) when the section 10(b) right to counsel was triggered under R v Suberu and whether questioning before it breached that right; (4) how the section 24(2) analysis in Grant applies here, including how Le, R v Tim and R v Zacharias treat cumulative or downstream breaches; and (5) how the Ontario Court of Appeal has applied Le since 2019, comparing at least three of its decisions by outcome. Read at least eight decisions and quote Grant's definition of detention verbatim. State Canadian law as of 2026-06-30. Search Sources, read the relevant passages rather than whole judgments, and give a source chip with a paragraph or section pinpoint for every legal proposition. Write about 800 words in this chat.

## Constraints

- Open source: search and read Canadian sources in Beaver (A2AJ case law and legislation, journals, Hansard).
- Every legal proposition carries a source chip with a paragraph or section pinpoint.
- Quotations are verbatim.
- Jurisdiction: Canada, as the prompt states. State the law as of 2026-06-30.
- Deliverable: about 800 words in the chat; then the research is organized into a workspace and a table.
