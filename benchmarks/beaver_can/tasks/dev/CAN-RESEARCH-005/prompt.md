# CAN-RESEARCH-005 — Open-source research memorandum

The duty to consult a First Nation on a federally regulated pipeline. Kind: public-law question reaching statutes, appellate decisions and a journal or Hansard source (Aboriginal law).

You are assisting counsel to a First Nation in northern Alberta whose treaty territory is crossed by a proposed federally regulated pipeline. Prepare a research memorandum for supervising counsel answering: (1) when the Crown's duty to consult is triggered under Haida Nation and Rio Tinto Alcan v Carrier Sekani, and whether a regulator's approval decision is Crown conduct that triggers it under Clyde River and Chippewas of the Thames; (2) where on the Haida spectrum this consultation must fall and what meaningful consultation and accommodation require, contrasting the Federal Court of Appeal's Tsleil-Waututh Nation and Coldwater First Nation decisions on the Trans Mountain expansion; (3) whether the Nation can insist on consent or a veto under Ktunaxa Nation, and whether the duty attaches to Parliament's enactment of the enabling legislation under Mikisew Cree First Nation v Canada (Governor General in Council), 2018 SCC 40; (4) what the United Nations Declaration on the Rights of Indigenous Peoples Act, SC 2021, c 14, and the Impact Assessment Act's provisions on Indigenous knowledge and rights add, citing the sections; and (5) one journal article or Hansard debate available in Sources that bears on the question, with its actual pinpoint. Read at least eight sources and quote Haida's statement of when the duty arises verbatim. State Canadian law as of 2026-06-30. Search Sources, read the relevant passages rather than whole judgments, and give a source chip with a paragraph or section pinpoint for every legal proposition. Write about 800 words in this chat.
