# CAN-CITE-001 — Quotation, pinpoint, and table of authorities mechanics

From the supplied source packet **only**, prepare a short memorandum
(`answer.docx`, maximum 800 words) that does all of the following:

1. Quotes **verbatim** the Criminal Code provision under which murder is first
   degree murder — irrespective of planning and deliberation — when the death
   is caused while committing kidnapping or forcible confinement, with a
   section-level pinpoint.
2. Quotes **verbatim** the Saskatchewan Court of Appeal's reasoning, as
   recounted by the Supreme Court of Canada in *R. v. Latimer*, for reversing
   the trial judge's constitutional exemption from the mandatory minimum
   sentence, with a paragraph pinpoint.
3. States in one sentence whether the packet authorities permit a sentencing
   court to exempt an offender from the mandatory minimum sentence for murder
   on compassionate grounds.
4. Ends with a heading **Table of Authorities** listing every authority cited
   in the memorandum with its citation and every pinpoint used.

## Constraints

- **Closed source packet.** Rely on and cite only the sources listed below.
- Every quotation must reproduce the source text exactly; every citation must
  carry a pinpoint (paragraph number for cases, section label for
  legislation).
- Do not list any authority in the Table of Authorities that the memorandum
  does not actually cite.

## Source packet

- SRC-001 — Criminal Code, RSC 1985, c C-46, s 231 (excerpt)
- SRC-002 — R. v. Latimer, 2001 SCC 1
- SRC-003 — R. v. Oakes, [1986] 1 SCR 103 (SCC)
