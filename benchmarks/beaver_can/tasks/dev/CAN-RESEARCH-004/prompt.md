# CAN-RESEARCH-004 — Open-source research memorandum

A departing senior employee, restrictive covenants and the election of remedies. Kind: private-law file mixing a statutory prohibition, fiduciary and good-faith duties, breach of confidence and quantum.

You are assisting Ontario commercial litigation counsel.

Our client, Northbridge Process Systems Ltd., is a privately held industrial-automation firm in Hamilton with about 90 employees. In March 2023 it hired Karim S. as Vice-President, Operations. He was never a director and never held the office of chief executive officer, president or any other chief officer position. His written employment agreement, signed in March 2023, contains: a covenant not to "engage in any business in competition with the Company anywhere in North America" for 12 months after his employment ends; a separate covenant not to solicit "any customer, prospective customer or employee of the Company" for 12 months, with no geographic limit and with "prospective customer" defined to include anyone the Company "has at any time considered approaching"; and a clause providing that both covenants run from the later of the end of his employment and the date on which all consents required from the Company's lenders are obtained.

For the last five months of his employment he led the renewal negotiations for the client's largest contract, which is about 38 per cent of revenue. He resigned on 9 January 2026 without notice, incorporated a competing company three weeks later, and six weeks after that the customer awarded the renewal to his new company on terms close to the ones he had been negotiating for us. Our forensic accountant has found that in his last month he emailed to his personal account the customer list and the pricing model. Two of our project engineers resigned and joined him within a month. We want an interlocutory injunction, and the client wants to know whether it can claim the profits he and his company have made rather than prove its own lost profits.

Prepare a research memorandum for supervising counsel answering: (1) whether either covenant is enforceable — deal with the statutory prohibition in the Employment Standards Act, 2000 on covenants of this kind, its exceptions and how the Act defines the class of employee who falls outside the prohibition, citing the sections, and then, for whatever covenant survives the statute, the common-law requirements and the consequences of ambiguity and of a period whose end date depends on a third party's consent; (2) whether he owed a fiduciary duty although he was neither a director nor an officer, and whether taking the renewal after resigning breached it; (3) whether his silence about the state of the negotiations while still employed breached a duty of good faith in contractual performance, and how far that duty reaches; (4) whether taking the customer list and pricing model founds a separate claim and what it adds; and (5) remedies and quantum — whether the client can elect an account of the profits he and his company made instead of its own lost profits, over what period a court measures that relief, whether a claim to strip gains is available where the wrong is only breach of contract, whether punitive damages are realistic, and what the client must establish to obtain an interlocutory injunction.

Read at least ten sources: decisions from more than one level of court, the statutory provisions themselves, and at least one journal article. Quote verbatim the Supreme Court's statement of what follows, for enforceability, when a restrictive covenant is ambiguous. State Canadian law as of 2026-06-30. Search Sources, read the relevant passages rather than whole judgments, and give a source chip with a paragraph or section pinpoint for every legal proposition. Write about 1,000 words in this chat.

## Constraints

- The prompt names no authority. Every governing case must be found by searching.
- Every legal proposition carries a source chip with a paragraph or section pinpoint.
- Quotations are verbatim.
- Jurisdiction: Ontario and Canada, as the prompt states. State the law as of 2026-06-30.
- Deliverable: about 1,000 words in the chat over at least 10 distinct sources; then the research is organized into a workspace and a table.
