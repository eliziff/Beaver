# CAN-RESEARCH-004 — Open-source research memorandum

Termination clause enforceability, reasonable notice and statutory minimums. Kind: statute-heavy question mixing two provinces' legislation and a regulation with case law (employment).

You are assisting Ontario employment counsel. Our client, an Ontario employer, intends to dismiss without cause a 54-year-old operations manager with eleven years' service earning $140,000 plus a discretionary annual bonus. Her contract limits her on termination to Employment Standards Act, 2000 minimums, contains a separate just-cause clause permitting dismissal without notice for cause, and contains a severability clause. Prepare a research memorandum for supervising counsel answering: (1) whether the termination clause is enforceable in light of Waksdale v Swegon North America, Rahman v Cannon Design Architecture and the Court of Appeal's later decisions, and whether the severability clause changes the result; (2) if it fails, how common-law reasonable notice is assessed for her and whether the bonus is recoverable during the notice period under Matthews v Ocean Nutrition Canada; (3) her statutory minimum entitlements under the Employment Standards Act, 2000 and O Reg 288/01: notice or pay in lieu, severance pay and its thresholds, and the wilful-misconduct exemption, citing the sections; and (4) how the minimum termination notice would differ had she worked in Calgary under Alberta's Employment Standards Code, citing its sections. Read at least six decisions and the statutory sections themselves, and quote Waksdale's holding that termination provisions are read as a whole verbatim. State Canadian law as of 2026-06-30. Search Sources, read the relevant passages rather than whole judgments, and give a source chip with a paragraph or section pinpoint for every legal proposition. Write about 800 words in this chat.
