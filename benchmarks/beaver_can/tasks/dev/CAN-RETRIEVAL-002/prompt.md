# CAN-RETRIEVAL-002 — Authority retrieval and ranking

Our client, an Ontario corporation, retained an accountant to prepare its
financial statements for fiscal 2019. In 2024 the client discovered, on
receiving a review by a second firm, that the 2019 statements had been
negligently prepared and materially misstated the company's position. The
client wants to sue the accountant in negligence and asks when the basic
limitation period on that claim started running.

From the supplied source packet **only**, identify the authorities that govern
when the basic limitation period started running on the client's claim, rank
them by relevance, and give pinpoint support (paragraph number for cases,
section label for legislation) for the governing proposition. Exclude packet
sources that do not bear on the limitation question.

## Constraints

- **Closed source packet.** Consider only the sources listed below; do not
  introduce outside authorities.
- Every entry in the ranking must give at least one pinpoint that actually
  supports the stated proposition.
- Jurisdiction: Ontario, law as of 2026-06-30.
- Deliverable: a ranked list saved as `answer.json`, each entry carrying
  `source_id`, `rank`, `proposition`, and `pinpoints`.

## Source packet

- SRC-001 — Limitations Act, 2002, SO 2002, c 24, Sch B (excerpt)
- SRC-002 — Grant Thornton LLP v. New Brunswick, 2021 SCC 31
- SRC-003 — Limitation Act, SBC 2012, c 13 (excerpt)
- SRC-004 — Real Property Limitations Act, RSO 1990, c L.15 (excerpt)
- SRC-005 — R. v. Oakes, [1986] 1 SCR 103 (SCC)
- SRC-006 — Food and Drug Regulations, CRC, c 870 (excerpt)
