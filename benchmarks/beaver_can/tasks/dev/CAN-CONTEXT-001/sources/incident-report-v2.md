## Narrative

Mr. P. Morin was injured after business hours, not on the shop floor. He
climbed through a gap beside the locked gate of the fenced storage yard behind
the store to retrieve a football his son had kicked over the fence. The gate
carries a sign reading "DANGER — STORAGE YARD — NO PUBLIC ACCESS". Inside the
yard, Mr. Morin climbed onto a stack of palletized canoes, which shifted, and
he fell. Staff had locked the gate at closing per procedure.
