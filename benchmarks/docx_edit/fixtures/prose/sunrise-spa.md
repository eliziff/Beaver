SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT made as of March 3, 2026 between SUNRISE HOLDINGS INC. (the "Vendor") and MERIDIAN CAPITAL PARTNERS LP (the "Purchaser").

ARTICLE I — DEFINITIONS

1.01 Defined Terms. In this Agreement: "Agreement" means this share purchase agreement including its schedules; "Business Day" means a day other than a Saturday, Sunday or statutory holiday in Ontario; "Closing" means the completion of the purchase and sale of the Purchased Shares; "Closing Date" means April 30, 2026 or such other date as the parties agree in writing; "Indemnified Party" means a Person entitled to indemnification under Article VIII; "Losses" means all losses, damages, liabilities, costs and expenses, including reasonable legal fees; "Person" means an individual, corporation, partnership, trust or other entity; "Purchased Shares" means all of the issued and outstanding common shares in the capital of the Corporation.

1.02 Interpretation. Headings are inserted for convenience only and do not affect construction. Words importing the singular include the plural and vice versa.

1.03 Currency. All references to dollars are to Canadian dollars unless expressly stated otherwise.

1.04 Schedules. Schedule A and Schedule B form part of this Agreement.

ARTICLE II — PURCHASE AND SALE

2.01 Purchased Shares. Subject to the terms of this Agreement, the Vendor shall sell and the Purchaser shall purchase the Purchased Shares, free and clear of all liens.

2.02 Purchase Price. The aggregate purchase price for the Purchased Shares is $12,400,000, allocated among the Purchased Shares as set out in Schedule A.

2.03 Payment. The Purchaser shall pay the purchase price on the Closing Date by wire transfer of immediately available funds, less the Escrow Amount withheld under Schedule B.

2.04 Closing. Closing shall take place at 10:00 a.m. on the Closing Date at the offices of the Vendor's counsel, or by electronic exchange of documents.

ARTICLE III — REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF THE VENDOR

3.01 Organization. The Corporation is a corporation duly incorporated and validly existing under the laws of Ontario.

3.02 Title to Shares. The Vendor is the registered and beneficial owner of the Purchased Shares, free and clear of all liens, and has the capacity to transfer them.

3.03 Financial Statements. The financial statements of the Corporation for the year ended December 31, 2025 present fairly its financial position, and record revenue of $12,400,000 for that year.

3.04 Taxes. The Corporation has filed all tax returns required to be filed and has paid all taxes shown as due on those returns.

3.05 Litigation. Except for the supplier claim described in Schedule A, in which the claimant seeks $2,000,000 in damages, there is no action or proceeding pending or, to the Vendor's knowledge, threatened against the Corporation.

3.06 Environmental. The Corporation holds all environmental permits required to operate the Business and is in material compliance with them.

3.07 Employees. Schedule C lists each employee of the Corporation with a base salary exceeding $100,000, together with that employee's position, date of hire and notice entitlement. No employee is represented by a trade union and no application for certification is pending.

3.08 Material Contracts. Schedule C lists each contract of the Corporation that involves annual payments exceeding $250,000 or that cannot be terminated on ninety days' notice without penalty. Each such contract is in good standing and no party to it is in material default.

3.09 Intellectual Property. The Corporation owns or is licensed to use all intellectual property used in the Business, and to the Vendor's knowledge the operation of the Business does not infringe the intellectual property rights of any Person.

3.10 Insurance. The Corporation maintains policies of insurance that are reasonable for a business of its size and nature, all premiums are paid to date, and no claim exceeding $100,000 is outstanding under any policy.

3.11 Real Property. The Corporation does not own real property. The leased premises listed in Schedule C are held under leases that are in good standing, and no landlord has given notice of default.

3.12 Books and Records. The corporate records of the Corporation are complete in all material respects and record all meetings and resolutions of its directors and shareholders.

3.13 No Undisclosed Liabilities. The Corporation has no liabilities other than those recorded in the financial statements referred to in Section 3.03, those incurred in the ordinary course since December 31, 2025, and those disclosed in Schedule C.

3.14 Compliance with Laws. The Corporation is conducting the Business in compliance in all material respects with applicable laws, and has not received written notice of any alleged violation that remains outstanding.

ARTICLE IV — REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF THE PURCHASER

4.01 Organization. The Purchaser is a limited partnership validly existing under the laws of Ontario.

4.02 Authority. The Purchaser has full power and authority to enter into this Agreement and to perform its obligations under it.

4.03 Financing. The Purchaser has sufficient committed funds available to pay the purchase price on the Closing Date.

ARTICLE V — COVENANTS

5.01 Conduct of Business. Until Closing, the Vendor shall cause the Corporation to carry on the Business in the ordinary course consistent with past practice.

5.02 Access. Until Closing, the Vendor shall give the Purchaser and its advisers reasonable access to the books and records of the Corporation during normal business hours.

5.03 Confidentiality. Each party shall keep confidential all information received from the other party in connection with this Agreement and shall use it only to evaluate and complete the transactions contemplated here.

5.04 Non-Competition. For three years after the Closing Date, the Vendor shall not carry on a business that competes with the Business anywhere in Ontario.

5.05 Notification of Breach. Each party shall promptly notify the other in writing on becoming aware of any fact that causes a representation given by it to be untrue in a material respect, and the notice does not cure the breach or affect the rights of the other party under Article VIII.

5.06 Exclusivity. Until the earlier of Closing and the termination of this Agreement, the Vendor shall not solicit, encourage or entertain any offer from a third party for the Purchased Shares or for a material part of the assets of the Corporation.

5.07 Employee Matters. The Purchaser shall cause the Corporation to continue the employment of each employee listed in Schedule C on terms no less favourable in the aggregate for a period of twelve months after the Closing Date.

5.08 Insurance Run-Off. The Vendor shall obtain and pay for a six year run-off endorsement on the directors' and officers' liability policy of the Corporation, effective as of Closing.

ARTICLE VI — CONDITIONS OF CLOSING

6.01 Purchaser's Conditions. The obligation of the Purchaser to complete the purchase is subject to the representations in Article III being true in all material respects on the Closing Date.

6.02 Vendor's Conditions. The obligation of the Vendor to complete the sale is subject to the Purchaser having delivered the purchase price, and to the Vendor's aggregate liability under Section 8.05 being limited to $2,000,000.

6.03 Waiver. Either party may waive a condition in its favour by notice in writing given under Section 9.01.

ARTICLE VII — CLOSING DELIVERIES

7.01 Vendor's Deliveries. At Closing the Vendor shall deliver share certificates for the Purchased Shares duly endorsed for transfer, together with the resignations of the directors of the Corporation.

7.02 Purchaser's Deliveries. At Closing the Purchaser shall deliver the purchase price and a certificate confirming the representations in Article IV.

ARTICLE VIII — INDEMNIFICATION

8.01 Vendor Indemnity. The Vendor shall indemnify and hold harmless each Indemnified Party from and against all Losses arising out of any breach by the Vendor of a representation in Article III, subject to the limitations in Section 8.05.

8.02 Purchaser Indemnity. The Purchaser shall indemnify and hold harmless each Indemnitee from and against all Losses arising out of any breach by the Purchaser of a representation in Article IV.

8.03 Notice of Claim. An Indemnified Party shall give the indemnifying party written notice of a claim for indemnification within 30 days after becoming aware of the facts giving rise to it, and failure to give timely notice relieves the indemnifying party only to the extent it is prejudiced.

8.04 Defence of Third Party Claims. The indemnifying party may assume the defence of a third party claim by notice given within 15 days after receiving notice under Section 8.03. If the indemnifying party assumes the defence, the Indemnitee may participate at its own expense, and the Indemnitee shall not settle the third party claim without the written consent of the indemnifying party.

8.05 Limitations. The aggregate liability of the Vendor under this Article VIII shall not exceed $2,000,000, and no claim may be made unless the aggregate of all Losses claimed by the Indemnitee exceeds $50,000.

8.06 Survival. The representations in Article III survive Closing for 18 months, after which no Indemnified Party may bring a claim under Section 8.01 in respect of them.

ARTICLE IX — NOTICES

9.01 Notices. Any notice under this Agreement must be in writing and delivered personally, sent by courier, or sent by electronic mail to the address of the recipient set out in Schedule A, and is deemed received on the next Business Day.

ARTICLE X — GENERAL

10.01 Entire Agreement. This Agreement, together with its schedules, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior negotiations.

10.02 Assignment. Neither party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other party, which consent may not be unreasonably withheld.

10.03 Governing Law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of Ontario and the federal laws of Canada applicable in Ontario.

10.04 Counterparts. This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which is an original and all of which together constitute one agreement.

10.05 Further Assurances. Each party shall execute such further documents as the other party reasonably requires to give effect to this Agreement.

ARTICLE XI — TAX MATTERS

11.01 Pre-Closing Returns. The Vendor shall prepare and file, or cause to be prepared and filed, all tax returns of the Corporation for taxation years ending on or before the Closing Date, and shall provide draft returns to the Purchaser at least thirty days before filing.

11.02 Straddle Period. For a taxation year that includes but does not end on the Closing Date, taxes are allocated between the parties on the basis of an interim closing of the books as at the end of the Closing Date.

11.03 Tax Indemnity. The Vendor shall indemnify the Purchaser for taxes of the Corporation attributable to a period ending on or before the Closing Date to the extent those taxes exceed the tax provision recorded in the financial statements referred to in Section 3.03. A claim under this Section 11.03 is subject to the limitations in Section 8.05.

11.04 Tax Contests. The Purchaser shall give the Vendor notice of any audit or assessment that could give rise to a claim under Section 11.03 within fifteen days after receiving it, and the Vendor may participate in the contest at its own expense.

11.05 Refunds. Any refund of taxes of the Corporation relating to a period ending on or before the Closing Date belongs to the Vendor, and the Purchaser shall pay it over within ten Business Days after receipt.

11.06 Cooperation. Each party shall give the other reasonable access to records and personnel as required to prepare returns, respond to audits, and pursue refunds under this Article XI.

ARTICLE XII — DISPUTE RESOLUTION

12.01 Negotiation. A party asserting a dispute under this Agreement shall give the other party written notice describing the dispute, and senior representatives of the parties shall meet within fifteen Business Days to attempt to resolve it.

12.02 Mediation. If the dispute is not resolved within thirty days after the notice under Section 12.01, either party may refer it to mediation before a single mediator agreed by the parties, with the costs of the mediator shared equally.

12.03 Arbitration. A dispute that is not resolved by mediation within sixty days after the referral shall be finally determined by arbitration before a single arbitrator under the Arbitration Act, 1991 (Ontario), seated in Toronto and conducted in English.

12.04 Interim Relief. Nothing in this Article XII prevents a party from applying to a court for interim or interlocutory relief to preserve the subject matter of the dispute or to enforce the confidentiality obligations in Section 5.03.

12.05 Continuing Performance. Each party shall continue to perform its obligations under this Agreement while a dispute is being resolved under this Article XII, except for the obligation that is the subject of the dispute.

ARTICLE XIII — ESCROW AND SET-OFF

13.01 Escrow Agent. The parties shall appoint Lakeshore Trust Corporation as escrow agent on the terms set out in Schedule B, and shall each pay one half of the escrow agent's fees.

13.02 Deposit of Escrow Amount. On Closing the Purchaser shall pay the Escrow Amount directly to the escrow agent rather than to the Vendor, and the payment discharges the Purchaser's obligation under Section 2.03 to that extent.

13.03 Release. The escrow agent shall release the balance of the Escrow Amount to the Vendor on the date that is eighteen months after the Closing Date, less the amount of any claim then outstanding and notified under Section 8.03.

13.04 Set-Off. The Purchaser may set off against the Escrow Amount any amount finally determined to be owing to it under Article VIII, and the exercise of set-off does not limit any other remedy available to the Purchaser.

13.05 Interest. Interest earned on the Escrow Amount accrues to the Vendor and is paid out with the balance released under Section 13.03.

13.06 Disputes. A dispute between the parties about the release of the Escrow Amount is a dispute under Article XII, and the escrow agent shall hold the disputed amount until it receives a joint direction or a final order.

ARTICLE XIV — CONFIDENTIALITY AND ANNOUNCEMENTS

14.01 Public Announcements. Neither party shall issue a press release or other public announcement about the transactions contemplated by this Agreement without the prior written approval of the other party, except as required by applicable law or the rules of a stock exchange.

14.02 Required Disclosure. A party required by law to disclose confidential information of the other party shall, to the extent permitted, give the other party notice sufficient to allow it to seek a protective order, and shall disclose only the portion of the information it is legally required to disclose.

14.03 Return of Information. On termination of this Agreement without Closing, each party shall return or destroy the confidential information of the other party and, on request, certify that it has done so.

14.04 Survival of Confidentiality. The obligations in Section 5.03 and this Article XIV survive Closing or termination for three years, and the obligations in respect of personal information survive indefinitely.

ARTICLE XV — TERMINATION

15.01 Termination Events. This Agreement may be terminated before Closing by mutual written agreement of the parties; by either party if Closing has not occurred by June 30, 2026 otherwise than through that party's default; or by either party if a condition in its favour under Article VI has become incapable of satisfaction.

15.02 Effect of Termination. On termination under Section 15.01 this Agreement is of no further force or effect, except that Section 5.03, Article XIV and this Article XV survive, and termination does not relieve a party of liability for a breach occurring before termination.

15.03 Return of Deposit. Any deposit paid by the Purchaser before Closing is repayable within five Business Days after termination unless the termination arises from the Purchaser's default, in which case the deposit is forfeited as liquidated damages and not as a penalty.

ARTICLE XVI — REGULATORY APPROVALS

16.01 Competition Act. If the transactions contemplated by this Agreement are a notifiable transaction under Part IX of the Competition Act (Canada), the parties shall each file the information required of it within ten Business Days after the date of this Agreement and shall request an advance ruling certificate or a no action letter.

16.02 Investment Canada Act. If a filing is required under the Investment Canada Act, the Purchaser shall prepare and submit it, and the Vendor shall provide the information about the Corporation reasonably required to complete the filing.

16.03 Cooperation on Approvals. Each party shall use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain the regulatory approvals required to complete the transactions, shall keep the other informed of the status of each filing, and shall not agree to an extension of a statutory waiting period without the consent of the other party.

16.04 Remedies and Divestitures. Nothing in this Article XVI requires the Purchaser to agree to a divestiture, hold separate arrangement, or other remedy that would be materially adverse to the Purchaser or to the Business taken as a whole.

16.05 Costs of Filings. Filing fees payable to a regulator in connection with the approvals referred to in this Article XVI are borne by the Purchaser, and each party bears its own professional fees in connection with the filings.

ARTICLE XVII — MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS

17.01 Time of the Essence. Time is of the essence of this Agreement, and no extension or waiver of a time limit is effective unless it is in writing and signed by the party granting it.

17.02 No Third Party Beneficiaries. Except as expressly provided in Article VIII in respect of an Indemnified Party who is not a party to this Agreement, nothing in this Agreement confers a right on any Person who is not a party to it.

17.03 Severability. If a provision of this Agreement is held to be invalid or unenforceable, that provision is severed and the remaining provisions continue in full force, provided that the severance does not defeat the commercial purpose of this Agreement.

17.04 Expenses. Except as expressly provided otherwise, each party bears its own legal, accounting and advisory expenses in connection with the negotiation and completion of the transactions contemplated by this Agreement.

17.05 Amendment and Waiver. No amendment of this Agreement is effective unless it is in writing and signed by both parties, and no waiver of a provision is effective unless it is in writing and signed by the party granting the waiver. A waiver on one occasion is not a waiver on any other occasion.

17.06 Language. The parties have required that this Agreement and all related documents be drawn up in English. Les parties ont exige que la presente convention et tous les documents connexes soient rediges en anglais.

Schedule F — Working Capital Adjustment Mechanics

Within sixty days after the Closing Date the Purchaser shall prepare and deliver to the Vendor a statement of the working capital of the Corporation as at the close of business on the day immediately before the Closing Date, prepared in accordance with the accounting principles used in preparing the financial statements referred to in Section 3.03 and applied on a basis consistent with them. Working capital means current assets less current liabilities, excluding cash, indebtedness for borrowed money, and income tax assets and liabilities. The Vendor has thirty days after receiving the statement to give the Purchaser written notice of an objection, specifying the item objected to and the basis of the objection in reasonable detail, failing which the statement is final and binding. If the Vendor gives a notice of objection, the parties shall attempt in good faith to resolve the objection within twenty Business Days, and any item that remains unresolved is referred to an independent firm of chartered professional accountants agreed by the parties, whose determination is final and binding absent manifest error and whose fees are borne by the parties in proportion to the amounts by which their respective positions differ from the determination. If final working capital exceeds the target working capital of $4,100,000, the Purchaser shall pay the excess to the Vendor within five Business Days after the statement becomes final; if final working capital is less than the target, the Vendor shall pay the shortfall to the Purchaser within the same period, and the Purchaser may set off the shortfall against the Escrow Amount under Section 13.04.
