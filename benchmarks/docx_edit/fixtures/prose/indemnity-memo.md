Re: Sunrise share purchase — indemnity position summary

This memorandum summarises the indemnity package the Purchaser obtained in the Sunrise share purchase agreement and flags the two points the business team asked about.

Scope of the vendor indemnity. The Vendor indemnifies the Purchaser side for losses arising out of a breach of the Vendor's representations. The indemnity is subject to a cap and to a claims threshold, and it is the Purchaser's only contractual recourse for a representation breach short of fraud.

Cap and threshold. The Vendor's aggregate liability under the indemnity article is capped at $2.5 million, and no claim may be advanced until the aggregate of claimed losses exceeds $50,000. The cap is a hard ceiling; it is not reduced by amounts recovered under the escrow.

Survival. The Vendor's representations survive closing for 24 months, after which no further claim may be brought in respect of them. The business team should diarise the expiry date and review open matters at least sixty days before it.

Notice mechanics. A claim must be notified in writing within 30 days after the claimant becomes aware of the facts giving rise to it. Late notice does not defeat the claim outright; it reduces recovery only to the extent the indemnifying party is prejudiced.

Escrow interaction. A separate escrow of $500,000 is held for eighteen months following closing and is released on joint direction or a court order. The escrow is a source of payment, not an additional limit.

Recommendation. The package is within market for a transaction of this size. We recommend the business team confirm the diary entries described above and raise no further points on the indemnity article.
