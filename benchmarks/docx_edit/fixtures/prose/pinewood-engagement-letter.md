Re: Engagement to advise on the Harbourfront lease renewal

Thank you for asking us to act for Cedar & Vine Restaurants Inc. on the renewal of your Harbourfront lease. This letter sets out the terms on which we will act, and we ask that you confirm your agreement by signing and returning the enclosed copy.

Scope of the work. We will review the existing lease and the renewal notice, advise on the renewal rent and the operating cost provisions, negotiate the renewal documents with the landlord's solicitors, and settle the final form of renewal agreement. We will not advise on tax structuring, employment matters, or any dispute that has already been commenced, unless you ask us in writing to expand the engagement.

Responsible lawyer and fees. Devon Achebe will have carriage of the matter and will bill at an hourly rate of $475. Time recorded by other lawyers and by law clerks is billed at the rates shown in the schedule below. Our rates are reviewed each January and we will give you thirty days' written notice before any change takes effect.

Disbursements and taxes. We will bill disbursements at cost and add harmonized sales tax to fees and taxable disbursements. Search fees, registration costs, and courier charges are the disbursements most likely to arise on a renewal of this kind.

Estimate. On the assumption that the landlord accepts the renewal rent without an arbitration, we estimate total fees of $18,000 to $24,000 plus disbursements and tax. We will tell you promptly if it becomes likely that fees will exceed the upper end of that range.

Accounts and payment. We render accounts monthly and they are payable on receipt. Interest accrues on overdue accounts at the rate permitted by the Solicitors Act.

Conflicts. We have searched our records and identified no conflict that prevents us from acting. If a conflict arises during the engagement we will tell you at once and discuss how to address it.

Termination. You may end this engagement at any time by written notice. We may withdraw where the Rules of Professional Conduct permit or require it, on reasonable notice to you.

Rate schedule. Partner, Devon Achebe: $475 per hour. Associate, Mira Halloran: $340 per hour. Law clerk, Josef Nowak: $195 per hour. Student-at-law: $145 per hour.

We are glad to be acting for you and look forward to getting the renewal settled well before the notice deadline.
