ARTICLE 1 — DEFINITIONS

1.01 Defined Terms. In this Agreement: "Base Rate" means, for any day, the greater of the Administrative Agent's announced prime rate for Canadian dollar loans in effect on that day and the one month CDOR rate for that day plus 1.00%; "Borrowing Notice" means a notice substantially in the form of Schedule 2; "Business Day" means a day other than a Saturday, Sunday or day on which banks are authorized to close in Toronto, Ontario; "Maturity Date" means January 15, 2031; "Revolving Facility" means the revolving credit facility described in Section 2.01; "Term Facility" means the term credit facility described in Section 2.03; "Total Commitment" means $85,000,000.

1.02 Accounting Terms. All accounting terms have the meanings given to them under IFRS as in effect from time to time.

[page 2]

1.03 Time. All references to a time of day are to Toronto time. Where a period is expressed in Business Days, the first day is excluded and the last day is included.

ARTICLE 2 — THE CREDIT FACILITIES

2.01 Revolving Facility. Each Revolving Lender severally agrees to make revolving loans to the Borrower from time to time in an aggregate principal amount not exceeding its Revolving Commitment set out in Schedule 1, up to a total of $35,000,000.

2.02 Borrowing Notice for Revolving Loans. The Borrower shall request a revolving advance by delivering a Borrowing Notice to the Administrative Agent not later than 11:00 a.m. three (3) Business Days before the proposed drawdown date, specifying the amount and the intended use of proceeds.

[page 3]

2.03 Term Facility. Each Term Lender severally agrees to make a single term loan to the Borrower on the Closing Date in the principal amount of its Term Commitment set out in Schedule 1, up to a total of $50,000,000.

2.04 Interest on Revolving Loans. Each revolving loan bears interest at the Base Rate plus a margin of 1.75% per annum, calculated daily and payable monthly in arrears on the last Business Day of each month.

2.05 Interest on Term Loans. The term loan bears interest at the Base Rate plus a margin of 2.00% per annum, calculated daily and payable monthly in arrears on the last Business Day of each month.

[page 4]

2.06 Voluntary Prepayment. The Borrower may prepay a revolving loan in whole or in part without penalty on giving the Administrative Agent notice not later than 11:00 a.m. three (3) Business Days before the proposed prepayment date.

2.07 Mandatory Prepayment. The Borrower shall prepay the term loan with the net proceeds of any disposition of assets outside the ordinary course exceeding $2,500,000 in any fiscal year.

ARTICLE 3 — FEES

3.01 Standby Fee. The Borrower shall pay a standby fee on the unused portion of the Revolving Facility at 0.45% per annum, payable quarterly in arrears.

[page 5]

3.02 Agency Fee. The Borrower shall pay the Administrative Agent an annual agency fee of $75,000, payable on the Closing Date and on each anniversary of it.

ARTICLE 4 — CONDITIONS PRECEDENT

4.01 Conditions to Closing. The obligation of each Lender to make its initial loan is subject to the Administrative Agent having received executed copies of this Agreement, the security documents, and a certificate of the Borrower as to its constating documents.

4.02 Conditions to Each Advance. The obligation of each Lender to make any advance is subject to no Event of Default having occurred and being continuing, and to the representations in Article 5 being true in all material respects.

[page 6]

ARTICLE 5 — REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES

5.01 Status. The Borrower is a corporation incorporated and validly existing under the Canada Business Corporations Act and is duly registered in each jurisdiction where the nature of its business requires registration.

5.02 Financial Statements. The audited consolidated financial statements of the Borrower for the fiscal year ended September 30, 2025 present fairly its consolidated financial position and were prepared in accordance with IFRS.

5.03 No Default. No Event of Default has occurred and is continuing, and no event has occurred that with notice or lapse of time would constitute an Event of Default.

[page 7]

ARTICLE 6 — COVENANTS

6.01 Financial Covenants. The Borrower shall maintain a ratio of consolidated total debt to consolidated EBITDA of not more than 3.50 to 1.00, tested quarterly on a rolling four quarter basis.

6.02 Reporting. The Borrower shall deliver to the Administrative Agent audited annual financial statements within 120 days after each fiscal year end and unaudited quarterly statements within 60 days after each fiscal quarter end, in each case with a Compliance Certificate in the form of Schedule 3.

6.03 Negative Covenants. The Borrower shall not, without the prior written consent of the Required Lenders, incur indebtedness for borrowed money other than the loans under this Agreement and purchase money obligations not exceeding $1,000,000 in aggregate.

[page 8]

ARTICLE 7 — EVENTS OF DEFAULT

7.01 Events of Default. Each of the following is an Event of Default: failure to pay principal when due; failure to pay interest within three (3) Business Days after it is due; breach of a financial covenant in Section 6.01; and the Borrower becoming insolvent.

7.02 Remedies. On the occurrence of an Event of Default that is continuing, the Administrative Agent may, and at the direction of the Required Lenders shall, declare all amounts owing under this Agreement immediately due and payable and enforce the security.

7.03 Notice of Default. The Administrative Agent shall give the Borrower notice of an Event of Default that has come to its attention not later than three (3) Business Days after becoming aware of it, but failure to give the notice does not waive the Event of Default.

[page 9]

ARTICLE 8 — THE ADMINISTRATIVE AGENT

8.01 Appointment. Each Lender appoints the Administrative Agent to act on its behalf under this Agreement and authorizes it to exercise the powers expressly delegated to it, together with such powers as are reasonably incidental to them.

8.02 Standard of Care. The Administrative Agent shall exercise the same care in the administration of the loans as it exercises in respect of loans made for its own account, and is not otherwise a fiduciary of any Lender.

[page 10]

ARTICLE 9 — MISCELLANEOUS

9.01 Notices. Notices under this Agreement must be in writing and are effective when delivered to the address or electronic mail address of the recipient set out in Schedule 1.

9.02 Amendments. No amendment of this Agreement is effective unless it is in writing and signed by the Borrower, the Administrative Agent and the Required Lenders.

9.03 Governing Law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of Ontario and the federal laws of Canada applicable in Ontario.
