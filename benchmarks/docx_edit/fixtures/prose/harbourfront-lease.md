HARBOURFRONT COMMERCIAL LEASE between HARBOURFRONT PROPERTIES LTD. (the "Landlord") and CEDAR & VINE RESTAURANTS INC. (the "Tenant").

PART 1 — DEFINITIONS AND GRANT

1.1 Definitions. In this Lease, "Premises" means Unit 300, 88 Harbour Street, Toronto; "Term" means the period of ten years commencing June 1, 2026; "Basic Rent" means the annual rent set out in Section 1.3; "Additional Rent" means all amounts other than Basic Rent payable by the Tenant.

1.2 Grant. The Landlord leases the Premises to the Tenant for the Term on the covenants set out in this Lease.

1.3 Basic Rent. The Tenant shall pay Basic Rent of $186,000 per year, payable in equal monthly instalments in advance on the first day of each month.

PART 2 — TENANT COVENANTS

2.1 quiet enjoyment. The Landlord covenants that the Tenant, paying rent and performing its covenants, shall have quiet enjoyment of the Premises during the Term without interruption by the Landlord.

2.2 REPAIR OBLIGATIONS. The Tenant shall keep the Premises in good repair, reasonable wear and tear excepted, and shall not permit waste.

2.3 Subletting. The Tenant shall not sublet the Premises or assign this Lease without the prior written consent of the Landlord, which consent may not be unreasonably withheld.

2.4 insurance. The Tenant shall maintain commercial general liability insurance with limits of not less than $5,000,000 per occurrence, naming the Landlord as an additional insured, and shall provide certificates of insurance annually.

2.5 SIGNAGE. The Tenant may install exterior signage in the location approved by the Landlord, and shall remove the signage and make good any damage at the end of the Term.

2.6 Utilities. The Tenant shall pay for all utilities separately metered to the Premises, and its proportionate share of utilities that are not separately metered.

PART 3 — LANDLORD COVENANTS

3.1 payment of taxes. The Landlord shall pay all realty taxes assessed against the building and recover the Tenant's proportionate share as Additional Rent.

3.2 Structural Repair. The Landlord shall maintain and repair the structure of the building, including the roof, foundation and load bearing walls.

3.3 Common Areas. The Landlord shall operate and maintain the common areas of the building in a manner consistent with comparable buildings in the area.

PART 4 — DEFAULT AND REMEDIES

4.1 Default. It is an event of default if Basic Rent remains unpaid for ten days after it is due, or if the Tenant breaches any other covenant and fails to cure within thirty days after written notice.

4.2 Remedies. On an event of default the Landlord may terminate this Lease, re-enter the Premises, and recover damages including the costs of reletting.

4.3 Notice. Notices under this Lease must be in writing and delivered personally or by courier to the Premises, in the case of the Tenant, and to the Landlord's head office, in the case of the Landlord.
