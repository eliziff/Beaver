# Pass marks for the pin test, set 2026-11-06 before any pin was scored

Each on samples no fix was tuned on, at least 1,000 pins per form.
A wrong answer is worse than no answer, so "right when answering" is the first bar.

| Form | Right when answering | Right of all pins whose quote is in our text | Must also |
|---|---|---|---|
| Page pins, first reporter | 99% | 95% | beat or tie the one-field reader |
| Parallel pins, second reporter | 99% | report as found, coverage is limited by the free data | beat the one-field reader by the pins it cannot answer |
| Vendor star pins | 99% | report as found | same |
| Paragraph pins | 99.5% | 97% | beat or tie the one-field reader |
| Footnote pins (n.3) | 99% | 95% | quote falls inside that note |
| Opinion parts (dissent, concurrence) | 99% | 95% | quote falls inside that writer's part |
| Headnote points | 99% | report as found | right point number |

A pin counts right if the quote starts or ends on the pinned page: a quote may cross a page.
Judges err too, so the marks stop short of 100%. Failures are read before they are blamed on the judge.
Pins excluded (no quote, altered quote, no resolved citation) are reported with their counts.
